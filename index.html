Terms of Service

Eightysix° Loyalty Program Terms

1. Program Overview: The Eightysix° Loyalty Program allows customers to earn 1 stamp per standard beverage purchase (or 2 stamps for double-dose items like Freddo Espresso).

2. Reward Redemption: Collecting 10 stamps unlocks 1 Free Coffee Reward. Rewards can be redeemed at participating Eightysix° locations or saved to your digital Rewards Wallet.

3. Account Security: You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials. Passwords must meet our minimum strength requirements (8+ characters, including an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, and a number).

4. Google Sign-In: If you choose to continue with Google, your account is created and matched using your Google name and email address. Your use of Google Sign-In is also subject to Google's own Terms of Service.

Privacy Policy

How Eightysix° protects your data

1. Data Collection: We collect only essential information (username/display name and, depending on how you sign up, an optional phone number, password, or Google account name and email) required to identify your account and sync your loyalty stamps across your devices.

2. Storage & Security: Data is encrypted locally and synced securely to Supabase Cloud infrastructure with anti-tampering checksums. Passwords are never stored in plain text — only a one-way cryptographic hash is kept, and it can't be reversed back into your password.

3. No Third-Party Sharing: Your personal information is strictly used for Eightysix° loyalty services and will never be sold or shared.

4. Signing In With Google: If you use "Continue with Google", we receive only your name and email address from Google to create and identify your card — never your Google password, and we never access any other part of your Google account.
